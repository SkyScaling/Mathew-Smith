Mathew Smith

Actuary · Life & Living Benefits Advisor

Hi, I'm Mathew. I help self-employed people and business owners across Ontario protect the one thing every other plan depends on: their income.

Before I was an advisor, I was an actuary. I spent years designing the life and living benefits products that get sold today. So I'm not reading you a brochure. I know how these plans are built, what they really pay out, and where the gaps hide.

Today I own an insurance agency that has served this community for close to 50 years, and I've been in the business for more than twenty.

I've watched it happen too many times. An illness or an injury stops someone from working, and a family that thought they were fine ends up in real trouble, because nobody protected the income everything else depended on.

My goal is to eliminate GoFundMe as a financial planning tool. I've seen too many good families end up there.

My team can also protect you, your family and your business, all under one roof, so nothing falls through the cracks between three different advisors.

On our call I'll ask about your work and your income. Then I'll show you black on white how much coverage you'd need and what it would cost. Then you decide, with no pressure from me.

Just know you can only put this in place while you're healthy. Once something happens, it isn't available at any price.

Outside of work, my wife is a doctor. Between the two of us, we spend most of our lives around people whose living depends on their own two hands. That's why this one matters to me.
